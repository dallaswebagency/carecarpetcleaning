Quality Carpet Cleaning Services in St. Albert

Since 1984, Al-Care Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning Inc has been offering high-quality carpet cleaning services in St. Albert, Edmonton and surrounding areas. From wall-to-wall carpet cleaning to fabric and area rugs cleaning, you can rely on us. Our professionals strive to clean your carpet and upholstery in an effective manner and ensure a long-lasting relationship with you. We also specialize in offering flood restoration services and restoring your household and office goods back to their former glory.
